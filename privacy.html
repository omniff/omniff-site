Omniff Privacy Notice

Last updated: 2026-11-07 · Omniff

In short

Omniff does not collect, send or share your data. The app collects no telemetry or usage statistics and asks for no account or sign-in. It makes one kind of internet connection: the one-time license activation described below, which only happens when you press Activate.

The app (desktop software)

  • Your file names, file contents, images, search queries and the search index are processed and stored only on your own computer. The AI models (visual/semantic search, labelling) ship with the app and run locally; no data is sent to any cloud service.
  • It reads files in the folders you choose and never modifies, deletes or moves your files.
  • Stored locally: the search index (the location you pick at install/in settings; default %LOCALAPPDATA%\com.projearama.app), a settings file and a diagnostic log (local only; you can open the folder from Settings).
  • To remove: remove folders in Settings (deletes their index) or answer yes to "also delete the index files" when uninstalling; you can delete the folders above for any leftovers.
  • If Windows lacks the Microsoft WebView2 Runtime, the installer may download it from Microsoft, which is subject to Microsoft's privacy terms.

License activation (the only network connection)

Omniff needs a free license key to run. You get the key from our website (our key provider, Gumroad, asks for your email address for that) and enter it in the app. When you press Activate, the app sends only your license key and our product ID to the license provider, which checks the key and counts the activation. The anonymous computer ID (a salted one-way hash of the Windows machine identifier) never leaves your computer; it only ties the saved license to that computer. Pressing Deactivate only deletes the saved license locally and sends nothing. Nothing else is ever sent: no file names, file contents, searches or usage data. After activation the app works offline and does not contact anyone again. The license provider processes this data under its own privacy policy.

Purchase

Keys are issued through Gumroad. Any payment, if a paid version is offered later, is handled by the sales platform named on our website; Omniff never sees or stores your payment details (card numbers etc.). The platform processes them under its own privacy policy. If you email us for support, we use your email address and message only to answer your request.

Website

Our website uses no tracking cookies or analytics tools.

Your rights and contact

Because the software does not process your personal data, we hold no such data to access or delete. For requests about support correspondence (including rights under applicable data protection law) contact omniff@proton.me.
